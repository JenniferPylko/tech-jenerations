# ∫A Proper Introduction

Now that you have shelter, sit down on your most comfortable item of furniture and let's talk about this modpack. This is a progression modpack focused on science and technology, with a (nearly) completely custom crafting tree and many additional mechanics.

Exploration is an important pillar of the pack with several added biome and structure mods. In addition to being an alternative to processing in some cases (e.g. fireclay can be found in the world or made with bauxite dust), some dimensions can only be accessed through certain structures found in the world. Chests may contain useful items, or notes with bits of lore and hints at progression.

Vanilla combat is entirely replaced with the Epic Fight mod. Weapons have weight to their swing and you can progress through a combat skill tree with xp.

## ∫More Things to Worry About

### ∫The Darkness

Night, caves, and weather can bring the darkness all the way to black. You won't be able to see anything without a light source. Some light sources, such as torches and lanterns, will burn out over time, so watch your fuel level.

### ∫Thirst, Water Quality, Temperature, and Air

In addition to hunger, you now experience thirst. Impure water can make you sick, so boil it over a campfire when you can. If necessary, flowing water is cleaner than standing water.

If the altitude's high or low enough you'll have difficulty breathing and slowly lose oxygen without a breathing apparatus. Other dimensions and planets may also have thin or no air.

You can die from freezing or overheating, so watch the heat and try to stay out of biomes that are too hot or too cold until you're able to craft insulated armor. You can craft a thermometer to see the exact temperature instead of a simplified representation.

### ∫They Can Hear You

Monsters are attracted to sound, so be careful out there.

### ∫Villagers Have Feelings

If you're caught stealing from a villager's chest or sleeping in a villager's bed, they won't like you very much.

### ∫Everything is Stressful

Everything that can transfer stress units also puts stress on the system. Shaft length isn't free anymore. On the other hand, machines use less stress when they're not active.
