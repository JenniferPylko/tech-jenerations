# The Copper Age

Once you've made a flint pickaxe, you'll be able to mine copper.

<Recipe id="charcoal_pit:flint_pick" />

To process the raw copper, you'll need to build a bloomery.

<Recipe id="charcoal_pit:bloomery_brick" />

With (char)coal and a block of raw copper you can make a copper bloom, then work it with a pickaxe to get copper ingots.

You'll now be able to craft copper tools. Copper pickaxes last significantly longer than stone pickaxes, making them essential for mining.
